Users ask messy questions. Rewrite them before you search. Part 9 of the RAG series 👇

→ Query rewriting: turn "what about India?" into a full, standalone search query
→ Multi-query: search with a few different wordings, then merge the results
→ Query decomposition: split a two-part question into two searches (built into Bedrock Knowledge Bases)
→ HyDE: have the LLM draft a quick answer, then search with that draft
→ Each trick costs an extra LLM call, so log both the original and the rewritten query
