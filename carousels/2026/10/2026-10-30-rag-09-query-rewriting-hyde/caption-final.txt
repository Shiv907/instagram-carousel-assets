Your users don't type clean search queries. They type "what about India?" and expect your RAG app to get it. Part 9 of the RAG series 👇

I've stopped sending the raw question straight to the vector search. Rewrite it first.

Query rewriting turns "what about India?" into a full, standalone query your retriever can actually use.

Multi-query searches with a few different wordings, then merges the results.

Query decomposition splits a two-part question into two searches. Bedrock Knowledge Bases has this built in.

HyDE is my favourite trick here. Let the LLM draft a quick answer first, then search with that draft.

One honest catch: every one of these costs an extra LLM call. So log the original query next to the rewritten one, or you'll never know which helped.
