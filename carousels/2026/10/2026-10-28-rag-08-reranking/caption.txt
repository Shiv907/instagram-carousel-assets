Retrieval finds candidates. Reranking picks the best ones. Part 8 of the RAG series 👇

→ Vector search is fast but rough, so near-misses sneak into your top results
→ A reranker reads the question and each chunk together, and scores how well they match
→ Retrieve a wide shortlist first, rerank it, then send only the best few to the LLM
→ In Amazon Bedrock you can call the Rerank API directly or turn on reranking in a knowledge base query
→ Models: Amazon Rerank 1.0 and Cohere Rerank 3.5 — Amazon Rerank 1.0 is not in us-east-1

Think hiring: skim 200 CVs fast, then interview the top 20 properly.
