Your vector search found the right chunk. It just ranked it too low, and the LLM never saw it. That's what reranking fixes. Part 8 of the RAG series 👇

Vector search is fast but rough, so near-misses sneak into your top results.

A reranker reads the question and each chunk together, and scores how well they actually match.

So the flow is: retrieve a wide shortlist, rerank it, then send only the best few to the LLM.

Think of hiring. You skim 200 CVs fast, then properly interview the top 20.

In Amazon Bedrock you can call the Rerank API directly, or turn on reranking in a knowledge base query. The models are Amazon Rerank 1.0 and Cohere Rerank 3.5. Heads up, Amazon Rerank 1.0 isn't available in us-east-1.
