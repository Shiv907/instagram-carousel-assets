Keyword search matches words. Semantic search matches meaning. Part 3 of the RAG series 👇

→ Keyword search finds documents that contain your exact words
→ Semantic search finds documents that mean the same thing, even with different words
→ "laptop won't turn on" needs semantic search to find "device fails to power up"
→ "invoice INV-20931" needs keyword search, because an ID has no meaning to match
→ Most real questions need both, which is where hybrid search comes in (part 5)

Think Ctrl+F vs asking a colleague who knows what you mean.
