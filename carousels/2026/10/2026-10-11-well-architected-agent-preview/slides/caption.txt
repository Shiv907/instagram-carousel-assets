AWS now has an AI architect for your cloud. Well-Architected Agent is in public preview 👇

→ Analyzes your AWS environment for cost, security, performance and resilience
→ You declare business goals; recommendations are prioritized by impact and effort
→ Three levels: resource findings, application findings, architectural patterns with IaC changes
→ Remediation paths: console walk-throughs, updated IaC templates, CLI commands, SSM runbooks
→ Review Terraform, CloudFormation or CDK before you deploy

Needs an AWS Support plan. Available in us-east-1, us-east-2 and us-west-2 (workloads can onboard from any commercial Region). Treat recommendations as drafts — GenAI can err, so evaluate before applying.
