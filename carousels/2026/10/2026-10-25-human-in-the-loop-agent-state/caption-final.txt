Let your agent act, just not alone. This is where I'd put human approval, and how to pause without losing state 👇

Pause before anything you can't undo: payments, deletes, emails, production changes.
Save the full state at the checkpoint: the history, the pending tool call and the budget used so far.
Let the human approve, edit or reject, then resume from exactly that point.

On AWS there are 4 common ways to interrupt: a Strands loop hook, inside the tool itself, a remote approver through Step Functions, or MCP elicitation.

Put a timeout on pending approvals, and re-check the world before you resume.
And don't ask for approval on everything, or people stop reading what they approve.
