Let your agent act, but not alone. Here's where to put human approval, and how to pause without losing state 👇

→ Pause before anything you can't undo: payments, deletes, emails, production changes
→ Save the full state at the checkpoint: history, the pending tool call, budget used
→ Let the human approve, edit or reject, then resume from exactly that point
→ On AWS there are 4 common ways to interrupt: a Strands loop hook, inside the tool, a remote approver via Step Functions, or MCP elicitation
→ Put a timeout on pending approvals and re-check the world before resuming
→ Don't ask for approval on everything, or people stop reading
