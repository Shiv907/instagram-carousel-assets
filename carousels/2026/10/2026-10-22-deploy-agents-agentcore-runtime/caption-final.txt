Your agent works on your laptop. Then real users show up. This is what changes when you deploy it 👇

Every user needs an isolated session with its own state. AgentCore Runtime gives each session its own microVM.
Some runs take minutes or even hours, so reply "started" and keep working in the background.
Reuse the same session ID. Otherwise each request can land on a fresh microVM and hit a cold start.
Keep state outside the process, set timeouts, and make your tools safe to retry.
Turn tracing on before launch, not after your first incident.

The new AgentCore Runtime (Sep 18) reclaims memory as a session goes cold, instead of billing you for the peak. In AWS's echo-agent test, the P75 cold start stayed around 2 s for images anywhere from 200 MB to 2 GB.
