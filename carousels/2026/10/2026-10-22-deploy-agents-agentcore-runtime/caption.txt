Your agent works on your laptop. Here's what changes when you deploy it 👇

→ Every user needs an isolated session with its own state. AgentCore Runtime gives each session its own microVM
→ Some runs take minutes or hours, so reply "started" and keep working in the background
→ Reuse the same session ID, or each request can land on a fresh microVM and a cold start
→ Keep state outside the process, set timeouts, make tools safe to retry
→ Turn tracing on before launch, not after the first incident

The new AgentCore Runtime (Sep 18) reclaims memory as it goes cold instead of billing the peak. In AWS's echo-agent test, its P75 cold start stayed around 2 s from a 200 MB to a 2 GB image.
