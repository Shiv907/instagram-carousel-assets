Smarter chunking: split documents by meaning, not by size. Chunking, part 2 👇

→ Semantic: cut where the topic changes, using embeddings to spot the shift
→ Hierarchical: retrieve small child chunks, then return their bigger parents for context
→ Contextual: add a short "where this came from" note to each chunk before embedding
→ Start simple and upgrade only when your evals show wrong or partial chunks

Think of a textbook: you find the exact paragraph, then read the section around it.

Bedrock Knowledge Bases has SEMANTIC and HIERARCHICAL built in. Contextual retrieval uses chunkingStrategy NONE plus a custom Lambda that enriches each chunk.
