Splitting docs every N tokens works until it slices an idea in half. Smarter chunking splits by meaning instead. Chunking, part 2 👇

Semantic chunking cuts where the topic changes, using embeddings to spot the shift.
Hierarchical chunking retrieves small child chunks, then hands back their bigger parents for context.
Contextual chunking adds a short "where this came from" note to each chunk before you embed it.

It works like a textbook. You find the exact paragraph, then read the section around it.

My advice: start simple. Upgrade only when your evals show wrong or partial chunks.

Bedrock Knowledge Bases has SEMANTIC and HIERARCHICAL built in. Contextual retrieval uses chunkingStrategy NONE plus a custom Lambda that enriches each chunk.
