How does a vector database search millions of vectors fast? It doesn't check them all. Part 4 of the RAG series 👇

→ Exact search compares your question with every stored vector. Too slow at scale
→ Approximate nearest neighbour (ANN) search finds very close matches with far less work
→ HNSW, a popular ANN index, is a layered graph: big jumps on top, small steps at the bottom
→ It's like finding a house: highway to the area, main road, then side streets
→ On AWS you can run vector search in OpenSearch Service, pgvector on RDS, MemoryDB, DocumentDB, Neptune Analytics or S3 Vectors
