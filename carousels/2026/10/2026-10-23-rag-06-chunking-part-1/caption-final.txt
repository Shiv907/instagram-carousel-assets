Your RAG system can only find what your chunks let it find. So how you cut your documents matters more than most people think. Chunking, part 1 👇

Your retriever finds chunks, not documents, and chunks with one idea each match best.

Fixed-size splits every N tokens. It's simple, but it can cut an idea in half.
Recursive tries paragraphs first, then lines, then words, until each piece fits.
Overlap repeats a little text at each edge, so a sentence sitting on the boundary survives whole.

Overlap isn't free, though. 20% overlap on a 10,000-token doc means 42 chunks instead of 34.

In Bedrock Knowledge Bases, fixed-size is one setting (maxTokens + overlapPercentage). The default is about 300 tokens and keeps sentences whole. For recursive splitting, use a custom Lambda.

It's like highlighting a textbook. Highlight a whole chapter and it helps no one. Highlight single words and you lose the meaning.
