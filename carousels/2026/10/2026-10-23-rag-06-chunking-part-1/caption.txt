How you cut your documents decides what your RAG system can find. Chunking, part 1 👇

→ Your retriever finds chunks, not documents. One idea per chunk matches best
→ Fixed-size: split every N tokens. Simple, but it can cut an idea in half
→ Recursive: try paragraphs first, then lines, then words, until each piece fits
→ Overlap: repeat a little text at each edge so a sentence on the boundary survives whole
→ Overlap isn't free: 20% overlap on a 10,000-token doc means 42 chunks instead of 34

In Bedrock Knowledge Bases, fixed-size is one setting (maxTokens + overlapPercentage). The default is about 300 tokens and keeps sentences whole. For recursive splitting, use a custom Lambda.

Think of highlighting a textbook: a whole chapter helps no one, and single words lose the meaning.
