Hybrid search: exact words and meaning, in one search. Part 5 of the RAG series 👇

→ Keyword search (BM25) catches exact codes, IDs and error messages
→ Semantic search catches meaning, even when the words differ
→ Hybrid runs both and fuses the two ranked lists into one
→ Score fusion normalises the scores first. RRF uses only ranks, so different score scales don't matter
→ Watch out: a doc only one list finds can sink, so give exact IDs their own fast path

In Bedrock Knowledge Bases it's one setting per query (overrideSearchType: HYBRID), on RDS, OpenSearch Serverless or MongoDB stores with a filterable text field. Other stores fall back to semantic search.

Like finding a song: type the exact title, or hum the tune. A good app takes either clue.
