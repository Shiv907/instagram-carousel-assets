Keyword search misses meaning. Semantic search misses exact IDs. Hybrid search gets you both. Part 5 of the RAG series 👇

Keyword search (BM25) catches exact codes, IDs and error messages.
Semantic search catches meaning, even when the words are different.
Hybrid runs both and fuses the two ranked lists into one.

There are two ways to fuse them. Score fusion normalises the scores first. RRF only looks at ranks, so different score scales don't matter.

One catch: a doc that only one list finds can sink to the bottom, so give exact IDs their own fast path.

In Bedrock Knowledge Bases it's one setting per query (overrideSearchType: HYBRID). It works on RDS, OpenSearch Serverless or MongoDB stores that have a filterable text field. Other stores fall back to semantic search.

Think of finding a song: you can type the exact title or hum the tune. A good app takes either clue.
