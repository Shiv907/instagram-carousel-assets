Embeddings turn meaning into numbers. Part 2 of the RAG series 👇

→ An embedding model turns a piece of text into a list of numbers, a vector
→ Texts with similar meaning get vectors that sit close together
→ "reset my password" lands near "I forgot my login", far from "pizza recipe"
→ Search then means finding the vectors closest to your question
→ Use the same embedding model for your documents and your questions

Think of a supermarket: similar things sit on the same shelf, so you find bread by walking to the bread aisle.
