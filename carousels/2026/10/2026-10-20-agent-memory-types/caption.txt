Your agent forgets everything after each session, unless you give it memory. Here are the types 👇

→ Short-term: the conversation so far, inside one session
→ Long-term: facts and preferences extracted and kept across sessions
→ Summaries: compressed history so the context window doesn't overflow
→ Episodic: important moments from past interactions, plus reflections on what worked

The context window is not memory. Memory is what you choose to extract, store and bring back.
