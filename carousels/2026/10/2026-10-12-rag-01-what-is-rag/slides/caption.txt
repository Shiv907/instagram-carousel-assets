RAG in one line: let the model look things up before it answers. Part 1 of a 16-part RAG series 👇

→ An LLM only knows its training data. It doesn't know your docs
→ RAG adds a lookup step: find the relevant pieces of your documents first
→ Those pieces go into the prompt, and the model answers from them
→ Setup happens once (chunk, embed, store). Lookup happens on every question

Think of a new support hire with the company handbook. They don't memorise it. They find the right page, then answer.

On AWS, Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases runs the whole pipeline for you.

Save this, the whole series builds on it 🔖
Follow @shivanshu_builds for all 16 parts. Part 2 (embeddings) is on Wednesday.
