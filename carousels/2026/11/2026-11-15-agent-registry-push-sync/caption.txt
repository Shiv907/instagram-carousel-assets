You deploy a new tool on your MCP server on Monday. Does your agent registry know on Tuesday?

AWS published a push-based way to keep AWS Agent Registry records fresh, so consumers stop reading stale tool schemas.

The flow:
CloudTrail sees UpdateAgentRuntime, EventBridge routes it, a Lambda calls tools/list on the server, diffs it against the record, and updates the record only if something changed.

The part I like: the update drops the record back to Draft until a curator approves it, so automation cannot skip review.

Know the limits before you copy it: it only updates records that are already APPROVED, and CloudTrail delivery takes about 5 to 15 minutes.
