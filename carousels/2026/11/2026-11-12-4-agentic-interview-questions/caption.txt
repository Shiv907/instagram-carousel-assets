Most agent interviews don't ask you to recite a framework. They ask how you recover when a tool fails, when a human must approve a write, when the tool list gets huge, and how you pick a decoding strategy.

I pulled 4 real questions from a public GitHub company-wise list (company attribution unverified) and wrote our model answers: what the interviewer is checking, plus a strong outline.

Exact questions:
1) How do you handle tool-call errors, timeouts and retries in an agentic loop?
2) Design human-in-the-loop approval for an agent that takes consequential actions.
3) How many tools is too many? How do you design tool schemas an LLM can actually use correctly?
4) Compare greedy, beam search, top-k, top-p and temperature sampling. When does each fail?

Which one would trip you up? Tell me in the comments, and save this before your next agent interview.
