Your agent demo works. Production is a different question.

That gap is exactly what this AWS post is about. On 9 Nov they published a seven-layer reference architecture for taking multi-agent apps on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore from prototype to production. To be clear, it's vendor guidance, not a standard, and the services it names are examples you can swap.

The seven layers, one per swipe:
1. Data and Knowledge
2. Model and Inference
3. Orchestration
4. Security and Guardrails
5. Application
6. Observability
7. Agentic Operations

The idea I liked most is stable contracts between layers. You can swap one technology without rebuilding everything around it.

Which layer does your team skip first? Tell me in the comments, and save this as a checklist for your next agent design review.
