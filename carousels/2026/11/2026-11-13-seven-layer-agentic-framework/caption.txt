Your agent demo works. Production is a different question.

AWS just published a seven-layer reference architecture for taking multi-agent apps on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore from prototype to production. It is vendor guidance, not a standard, and the services it names are examples you can swap.

The seven layers, one swipe each:
1. Data and Knowledge
2. Model and Inference
3. Orchestration
4. Security and Guardrails
5. Application
6. Observability
7. Agentic Operations

My favourite idea: stable contracts between layers, so you can change one technology without rebuilding the rest.

Save this as a checklist for your next agent design review. Which layer does your team skip first? Tell me in the comments.
