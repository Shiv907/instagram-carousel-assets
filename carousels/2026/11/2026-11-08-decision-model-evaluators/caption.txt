Your LLM judge doesn't need to read every turn. Let a calibrated model screen them first 👇

→ A decision model is a small, specialized model that picks from a fixed set of answers and returns a calibrated probability, not free-form text
→ Three question types: yes/no (noul), choice (up to 255 options), score (2 to 10 ordered levels)
→ In AgentCore Evaluations it plugs in as a code-based evaluator: a Lambda gets the agent's OpenTelemetry spans, calls the model, returns a label + value
→ The cascade: screen every turn with the decision model, escalate only the turns below your threshold to the built-in LLM evaluators for an explanation
→ LLM as judge: score + explanation in 2–10 s. Decision model: calibrated probability in ~hundreds of ms

Before you gate a release on it, validate on your own traffic: 3 human labelers, majority vote, then check agreement, calibration and disagreements. Pick a threshold per evaluator instead of defaulting to 0.5.

The ECE numbers on slide 7 are illustrative, so you can check the maths yourself.
