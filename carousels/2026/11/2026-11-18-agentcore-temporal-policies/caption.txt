Your agent's guardrails have amnesia 🧠

A normal Cedar policy is stateless. It only sees the current request, so it can't say "only disburse a payment after this claim was approved".

AgentCore temporal policies can. Their decisions depend on what happened earlier in the session.

What to know before you use them:
→ Written in Dogwood, which supports all existing Cedar policies
→ formerly within, since within, count and sum
→ Limits: 24h window, 3 operators per policy, 20 temporal policies per engine
→ The prior action needs its own plain permit, or the rule never matches
→ Roll out in LOG_ONLY first, then ENFORCE
→ You must send the session-ID header yourself
→ A count limit is not a security control: a new session resets it
