Your agent's guardrails have amnesia. A normal policy has no idea what the agent did five minutes ago.

Here's the problem. A standard Cedar policy is stateless, so it only sees the current request. That means you can't write a rule like "only disburse a payment after this claim was approved".

AgentCore temporal policies fix exactly that. Their decisions depend on what already happened earlier in the session.

What I'd want to know before using them:

They're written in Dogwood, which supports all your existing Cedar policies.

You get four operators: formerly within, since within, count and sum.

The limits are real: a 24h window, 3 operators per policy and 20 temporal policies per engine.

The one that would trip me up: the earlier action needs its own plain permit. Skip it and the rule never matches.

Roll out in LOG_ONLY first, then switch to ENFORCE.

You have to send the session-ID header yourself.

And don't treat a count limit as a security control. A new session resets it.
