Your "*" tools list just grew, and nobody asked you.

On Nov 11, AWS began rolling out Strands harness defaults to AgentCore harness. No customer action required. So if your harness leaves out allowedTools, or sets it to ["*"], it now picks up the new managed built-ins.

The new ones: todo_write, subagent, web_fetch, and separate read, write and edit tools.

Here's what I'd do about it.

If you need a fixed boundary, list the tools you actually need. AWS itself says the "*" set can gain capabilities over time.

Pin the model and system prompt as well, or they follow the managed defaults.

For anything consequential, add a before_tool_call Lambda hook. Only Lambda hooks can allow or deny, and failureMode defaults to deny.

The good news: subagent tool calls go through the same hooks, so delegating doesn't skip your checks.

Save this before your next harness deploy 🔖 Quick one for the comments: is your allowedTools a list or a star?
