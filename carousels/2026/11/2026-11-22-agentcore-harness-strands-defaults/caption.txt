Your "*" tools list just grew, and nobody asked you.

AWS began rolling out Strands harness defaults to AgentCore harness on Nov 11. No customer action required. If your harness omits allowedTools, or sets it to ["*"], it now gets the new managed built-ins.

The new ones: todo_write, subagent, web_fetch, and separate read, write and edit tools.

What I'd do about it:

If you need a fixed boundary, list the tools you actually need. AWS says the "*" set can gain capabilities over time.

Pin the model and system prompt too, or they follow the managed defaults.

Add a before_tool_call Lambda hook for anything consequential. Only Lambda hooks can allow or deny, and failureMode defaults to deny.

Subagent tool calls go through the same hooks, so delegation doesn't skip your checks.
