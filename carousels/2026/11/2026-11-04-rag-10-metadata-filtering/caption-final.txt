Filter first, then search. Part 10 of my RAG series, and honestly one of the most underrated fixes 👇

Metadata is just labels on each chunk: year, country, department, product, who can see it.

Filter on those labels first, so the vector search only returns chunks that match.

That's what stops "2026 leave policy for India" from pulling up the 2023 US policy. Similar text, wrong answer.

Bedrock Knowledge Bases lets you set filters per query, and it can even build them from the question itself.

My favourite use: permissions. Only search the chunks this user is allowed to see.

Think of it like ticking size and price on a shopping site before you start browsing.
