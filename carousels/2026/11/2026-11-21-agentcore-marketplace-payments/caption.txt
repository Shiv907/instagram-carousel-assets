Your agent can now buy things on your AWS bill.

Paid APIs usually mean a human signs up, adds a card and creates an API key. AgentCore payments now lets an agent pay x402-enabled AWS Marketplace sellers per request, in USD, with no crypto wallet.

The flow, as documented:

Discover with three read-only Gateway tools. Get a 402 quote. Call ProcessPayment with AWS_BILLING_X402. Retry with the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header.

What I'd check before using it:

The payment token is valid for 5 minutes and should be treated like a credential.

Sessions are where your spend cap lives. Don't give the paying role CreatePaymentSession, or it can mint a bigger budget.

A single payment must be above $0.00 and at most $10,000.00.

At launch it supports the x402 exact scheme only.
