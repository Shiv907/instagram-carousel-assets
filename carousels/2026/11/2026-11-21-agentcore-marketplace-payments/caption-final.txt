Your agent can now buy things on your AWS bill. That sentence made me sit up.

Normally a paid API means a human signs up, adds a card and creates an API key. With AgentCore payments, an agent can pay x402-enabled AWS Marketplace sellers per request, in USD, with no crypto wallet.

The flow, as AWS documents it:

Discover with three read-only Gateway tools. Get a 402 quote. Call ProcessPayment with AWS_BILLING_X402. Retry with the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header.

Before I'd turn this on, I'd check four things.

The payment token is valid for 5 minutes. Treat it like a credential.

Your spend cap lives in the session. So don't give the paying role CreatePaymentSession, or it can mint itself a bigger budget.

One payment has to be above $0.00 and at most $10,000.00.

At launch, only the x402 exact scheme is supported.
