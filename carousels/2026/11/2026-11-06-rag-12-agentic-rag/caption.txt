Agentic RAG: instead of searching once, the model decides how to search. Part 12 of the RAG series 👇

→ It breaks a complex question into smaller sub-questions
→ It searches, checks whether it has enough, and searches again if not
→ Retrieval becomes a tool inside the agent loop, next to your other tools
→ Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases does this with agentic retrieval (the AgenticRetrieveStream API)
→ The price: more LLM calls, so set a max iteration count and read the traces

A search box answers what you typed. A research assistant figures out what to look up.
