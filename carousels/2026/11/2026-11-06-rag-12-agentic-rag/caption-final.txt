Most RAG setups search once and hope for the best. Agentic RAG lets the model decide how to search. Part 12 of my RAG series 👇

Here's the shift as I see it.
It breaks a messy question into smaller sub-questions.
It searches, checks if it actually has enough, and searches again if it doesn't.
Retrieval stops being a fixed step and becomes just another tool in the agent loop.

If you're on AWS, Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases already does this with agentic retrieval (the AgenticRetrieveStream API).

The catch is real though. More LLM calls. So set a max iteration count and actually read the traces.

A search box answers what you typed. A research assistant figures out what to look up. That's the whole difference.
