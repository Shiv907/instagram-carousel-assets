Your Bedrock bill can keep climbing even after you've tuned the prompt.

AWS tested four request-level settings that change cost without changing your model or prompt:

→ Cache duration. On Claude Sonnet 5 and Opus 5, a 5-minute cache write costs 1.25x input, a 1-hour write costs 2x, and a read costs 0.1x. If the same prefix repeats with gaps over 5 minutes, the 1-hour cache wins from the second request in the hour.
→ Service tier. Flex on Nova 2 Lite was half the Standard rate and the bill matched. Flex on Sonnet 5 returned a ValidationException.
→ Reasoning effort. Same 24 of 24 correct runs, but median output tokens went from 936 at low to 2,521 at high (the default on Sonnet 5 and Opus 5).
→ maxTokens. A 155-token fence avoided 1,893 output tokens on a verbose prompt, but both responses were truncated.

My take: change one setting at a time, measure tokens, and track the task pass rate. A token saving that breaks the task is not a saving. The effort numbers are from one tool-calling agent test, so measure on your own tasks.
